Exoplanet Science Lab

Scientific modeling and uncertainty quantification in your browser. Plan an observation, look at your sky, reduce and fit the data, and check the models against other codes. Everything runs on your computer: nothing you load is sent anywhere.

Where to start

New here? Open Analyze and pick a worked example: a single transit, the radial velocities of HD 80606 b, three planets of TOI-1873, or a hidden planet found from transit timing. Coming from DS9, IRAF, AstroImageJ, EXOTIC, batman or RadVel? See how each part compares.
